About the Symposium

Fostering Innovation, Creating Leaders

SLICE v26 is the annual flagship National Level Technical Symposium organized by the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at Loyola-ICAM College of Engineering and Technology (LICET).

We host a series of intensive challenges testing coding, logic, circuit design, and entrepreneurial pitching. Join us to compete with the finest minds in the country, show your talent, and win exciting cash prizes!

Symposium Events

TIMELINE

Schedule of the Day

08:30 AM - 09:30 AM

Reporting & Registration

Verification of online tickets, on-spot entries check, kit distribution, and welcome breakfast.

09:30 AM - 10:15 AM

Inaugural Ceremony

Symposium inauguration at the college auditorium, address by college dignitaries, and briefing of events.

10:30 AM - 01:00 PM

Event Prelims & Workshops

Round 1 runs for all technical challenges (Code-X, Byte The Bomb, Astro Logiq, Cosmo-Connect). Hand-on workshops begin.

01:00 PM - 02:00 PM

Lunch Break

Buffet lunch provided for all registered symposium participants at the campus dining area.

02:00 PM - 03:30 PM

Final Rounds

Final rounds for gaming (Bingo-Nauts), circuit design, treasure hunt runs, and paper presentations.

03:30 PM - 04:30 PM

Valedictory Ceremony

Distribution of cash awards, medals, and certificates of achievement to winners and runners.
