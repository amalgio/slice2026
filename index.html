About the Symposium

Fostering Innovation, Creating Leaders

SLICE 2026 is the annual flagship National Level Technical Symposium organized by the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering. We bring together the brightest minds across the nation to learn, compete, and showcase innovative ideas.

Join us for a day filled with exciting technical events, hands-on workshops, and non-technical games loaded with cash prizes. Certificates of participation will be awarded to all registered attendees.

Schedule of the Day

08:30 AM - 09:30 AM

Reporting & Registration

Verification of tickets at the reception desk, kit distribution, and breakfast.

09:30 AM - 10:15 AM

Inaugural Ceremony

Welcome address, lighting of the lamp by the chief guest, and symposium briefing.

10:30 AM - 01:00 PM

Event Prelims & Workshops

Technical and non-technical event rounds start. Hands-on workshop session begins in the seminar halls.

01:00 PM - 02:00 PM

Lunch Break

Complimentary lunch for all participants at the college dining hall.

02:00 PM - 03:30 PM

Final Rounds

Grand finals for coding, gaming, and presentations. Workshop project submissions.

03:30 PM - 04:30 PM

Valedictory Ceremony

Distribution of cash prizes, shields for winners, participation certificates, and vote of thanks.
